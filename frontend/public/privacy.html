Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Blue Vertex runs this game and is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: wangdaqian08@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: an account id from Google or WeChat sign-in (or, for guests, the nickname you chose), your nickname and your profile picture address.
  • Your games: the rooms and games you played, your roles, results and statistics.
  • Your credits: your balance and a record of every credit added or spent.
  • Your purchases: order numbers, amounts, dates, status, and the ids Stripe gives the payment and your Stripe customer record.
  • When your account was created and last used.

Card details are entered on Stripe's payment page and handled by Stripe. They never reach our servers.

Why we use it

To run the game and your account, to process purchases and refunds, to keep records the law requires, and to prevent fraud and cheating.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, to process payments, refunds and disputes.
  • Google or WeChat, when you sign in with them.
  • Cloudflare, the network provider that carries traffic to the game.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Your browser

The game keeps your sign-in session and settings (such as sound) in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

Account and game information is kept while your account exists. Purchase records are kept for as long as Australian tax and accounting law requires (for example, five years).

Access and deletion

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account, by emailing wangdaqian08@gmail.com. Records we must keep by law are kept until that period ends.

See also our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.
