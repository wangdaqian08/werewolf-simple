Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Change of mind

You can get a full refund of a credit purchase if you ask within 14 days of buying it and you have not spent any credits since that purchase.

  • Credits you earn by playing do not count as spending.
  • A role-pick card whose credits were returned to you in full (because the draw missed or the game was cancelled) does not count as spending.
  • Each purchase is checked on its own: spending after a purchase means that purchase can no longer be refunded for a change of mind.

Example:

  • 1 November: you buy 330 credits (order A).
  • 3 November: you win 20 credits in a game. Order A can still be refunded.
  • 5 November: you use 25 credits on a role-pick card and get the role. From now on, order A cannot be refunded for a change of mind.

Outside these conditions we do not give refunds for a change of mind.

When something goes wrong on our side

Our goods and services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If you paid but did not receive your credits, were charged the wrong amount, or your credits do not work as they should, contact us and we will fix it or refund you, whether or not 14 days have passed and whether or not you have spent credits.

How to ask for a refund

Email wangdaqian08@gmail.com with the order number. You can find it in the game under 我的賬戶 → 充值記錄 (My account → Top-up history).

How a refund is made

Refunds are full refunds of the purchase, paid back to the original payment method through Stripe. It usually takes 5–10 business days to reach you. The credits from that purchase are removed from your account, even if this makes your balance negative.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank. If you dispute a payment, the credits from that purchase are removed while the dispute is open, and given back if the dispute is decided in our favour.
